Operator walkthrough

  1. Home / Today — Open Today to see what needs attention.
  2. Open a deal — Tap the top CALL TODAY card.
  3. Call supplier — Use the primary CALL SUPPLIER button.
  4. Record answers — Enter price, freight, lead time on one screen.
  5. Save and move on — Save Call, then work the next item.

Target: under 5 minutes. Full training: 15–30 minutes.
