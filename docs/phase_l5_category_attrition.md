# Phase L.5 — Category Attrition

Categories tracked in audit:

IT · TOOLS · FLEET · EQUIPMENT · MRO · SAFETY · LAB · OFFICE · OTHER

## Observation

Fleet / equipment / IT / MRO descriptive titles were heavily lost under L.4 (`no_identity_anchor`) and are the main L.5 recoveries via `descriptive_spec` / `brand_or_equal`.

Military specialty NSN titles continue to route to specialty (or mil-spec open when brand/MPN present) — not blindly admitted as commercial open.

Commercial retention rate (estimate): **2.3% → 12.4%** overall (commercial Stage 3 / commercial Stage 1 estimate).
