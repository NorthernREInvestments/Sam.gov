# M3 Operator Quickstart

**Read time: ~10–15 minutes**  
**Daily tool:** open `/ops` (Operator Console)

You do **not** need to understand research phases, adapters, or JSON stores.  
M3 tells you the next action.

---

## Daily workflow

1. Open **Home** — see what needs attention.
2. Open **Today** — your work queue.
3. Work top item first (usually **CALL TODAY**).
4. Do the one primary action.
5. **Save**.
6. Move to the next item.

That’s the whole job loop.

---

## Calls

1. Open a **CALL SUPPLIER** deal.
2. Confirm supplier, phone / RFQ link, product, quantity, delivery.
3. Ask **Must ask** questions (price, freight, delivery, lead time, terms, quote expiration).
4. Enter answers on the same screen while on the call.
5. Add notes.
6. If they promise a quote, set **Quote promised by** date.
7. Click **Save Call** (or **Mark Complete** when done).

You should see **Saved** with a timestamp.

---

## Quotes

1. Open **Quotes**.
2. Review **NEW QUOTES** / **NEEDS REVIEW**.
3. Check unit price, freight, landed total, lead time, terms, expiration.
4. Follow the recommendation: **BID**, **GET ANOTHER QUOTE**, **NEGOTIATE**, or **SKIP**.
5. Save.

Owner economics (profit, max-buy) are for owner review — do not share with suppliers.

---

## Registrations

1. Open **Registrations**.
2. Pick a **HIGH** priority portal.
3. Follow the walkthrough (where to go → what to enter → confirm).
4. Click **Mark Registered**.

Each card shows why the time is worth it (buyers / opportunities unlocked).

---

## Blocked

Blocked ≠ Watch.

Examples:

- **CAGE required** — federal access waiting on CAGE. Usually **no action inside M3**.
- **DIBBS registration** — cannot submit DLA yet.
- **Manufacturer authorization** — needs authorized source.

Read the blocker text. If it says no action inside M3, leave it and return to Today.

---

## Bid Prep / Response

When a deal is bid-ready:

1. Open **Bid Prep** (or Today → **RESPONSES**).
2. Review the deal summary and next action.
3. **BUILD RESPONSE PACKAGE** when the card says so (draft only).
4. **RUN FINAL PREFLIGHT** — see exact checks passed / blockers.
5. If preflight is clean, hand off to owner for **APPROVE FOR SUBMISSION**.
6. After approval, follow **guided submission** steps (portal / email / physical).
7. **Capture receipt** (confirmation number or file). Upload ≠ confirmed.

Never treat a draft package as submitted.

---

## Submission (guided)

1. Owner approved the package.
2. Open Bid Prep → Review & submit (or Today → **SUBMISSIONS**).
3. Follow checklist: open portal → upload listed files → enter approved price → stop for final submit.
4. Capture confirmation → deal moves to **AWAITING RESULT**.

---

## Owner handoff

Items under **OWNER ACTIONS** need the owner (attestations, signatures, final approval).  
As operator, leave them — do not invent answers.

---

## Watch

Future / weak / not worth resolving yet.  
Shown with **why watching** and **what moves it forward**.  
Do not mix with Blocked or Call Today.

---

## Training Mode

Toggle **Training** in the header for short hints (“Start here”, “Call this supplier”, …).  
Optional first-run walkthrough is under **Walkthrough**.

---

## Rules of thumb

- One primary button per deal.
- Save before leaving a screen.
- Ignore phase names and Advanced details unless an owner asks.
- Never invent portfolio totals when evidence is weak.
- Waiting (supplier / registration / award) is not failure.
- If save fails: retry — notes are preserved when possible.
- If a queue is empty: you’re caught up for that lane.
