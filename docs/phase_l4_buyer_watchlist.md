# Phase L.4 — Buyer Watchlist

Productive commercial buyers are persisted to:

`data/phase_l4_commercial_buyer_watchlist.json`

Status progression:

1. `COMMERCIAL_BUYER_WATCHLIST` — first qualifying commercial opportunity
2. `RECURRING_COMMERCIAL_BUYER` — ≥2 commercial/quote opportunities
3. Registration hint: `REGISTER_NOW_RECURRING_BUYER` (no outreach)

Platform peer expansion: when a buyer on Bonfire/OpenGov/etc. is productive, peer agencies on the same platform are queued for future discovery.
